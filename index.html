HOW IT WORKS

A 50% development deposit is required to begin. We develop the product and produce the first prototype for your approval. Once approved, the final unit price is calculated based on the finished design and required quantity.

If you proceed with a production order, the development fee is credited toward the total order value.

Larger production quantities can mean a lower unit cost. All custom projects are evaluated individually.

Discounts are available for Latvian companies working toward sustainability.
