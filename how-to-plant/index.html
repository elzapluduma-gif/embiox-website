A little guide for what comes next

Your Pen's Journey
Doesn't End Here.

If you're here, you may have bought an Embiox pen or received one as a gift. Either way, we're glad you're here. Your pen is part of a small step toward using less conventional plastic. Here's how to give its plantable part a chance at a second life.

From a daily note to a new beginning

Four simple steps

Use it, then let nature take it from here.

When the ink runs out, keep the plantable part and follow these steps.

01

Use it.

Write, create, sketch and let your ideas flow. Enjoy your Embiox pen just like any other pen until the ink runs out.

02

Take it apart.

When you're finished, remove the non-biodegradable parts, such as the ink refill. Keep them separate and dispose of them according to your local waste guidelines.

03

Plant it.

Place the plantable part about 5–10 cm beneath the soil. Cover it gently, then give the soil a little water.

04

Watch it grow.

With suitable growing conditions, you may see the first signs of growth in around two months. Offer a little water and sunlight, then give it time.

A note on growing

Seeds and growing conditions vary, so germination and timing can't be guaranteed. A little patience is part of the process.
